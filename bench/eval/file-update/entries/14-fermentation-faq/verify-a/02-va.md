# VA-14.02 — Koji temperature requirements

## Answer

28–32 °C. Below ~21 °C it fails (B-002 was a cold-room failure).

## Sources

- `raw/fermentation/recipes.md` — koji Method: "hold at 30–32 °C for 18 h" then "28–30 °C for 36–48 h". Notes: "The first attempt in 2023-06 failed due to a cold room (21 °C); see batch-log.xlsx B-002 notes."
- `raw/fermentation/ph-readings.csv` — B-018 readings show temperatures around 19–21 °C.

## Reasoning

The koji recipe specifies a two-stage temperature regime: 30–32 °C for the covered phase, then 28–30 °C for the tray phase, giving an overall range of 28–32 °C. The notes mention a failure at 21 °C, establishing the lower bound below which koji growth stops.

## Gotchas

The koji recipe has two temperature stages. Both fall within the 28–32 °C range. The cold failure threshold of ~21 °C is explicitly mentioned in the notes.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "28–32 °C; below 21 °C it won't grow"
- "28–32°C. Too cold (below ~21°C) causes failure as seen with B-002."
- "It needs 28–32 °C. If below about 21 °C, the fermentation fails."
