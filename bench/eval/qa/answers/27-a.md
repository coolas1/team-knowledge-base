# A27 — Ferment type with highest mean rating

## Answer

**Miso** has the highest mean rating at **4.2 / 5** (5 batches: ratings
4, 4, 4, 4, 5).

## Full table (all five types, ranked)

| Type | n | Ratings | Mean |
| ---- | --- | ------- | ---- |
| **miso** | 5 | 4, 4, 4, 4, 5 | **4.2** |
| koji | 4 | 2, 5, 5, 4 | 4.0 |
| kimchi | 6 | 5, 5, 2, 5, 3, 4 | 4.0 |
| kombucha | 2 | 3, 4 | 3.5 |
| hot_sauce | 3 | 2, 2, 3 | 2.3 |

## Sources

- `raw/fermentation/batch-log.xlsx` → sheet `Batches`, columns `type` and
  `rating_1to5`. All 20 batch rows.

## Reasoning

Group rows by `type`, average `rating_1to5` within each group:

- miso (B-002, B-008, B-010, B-011, B-018) → ratings 4, 4, 4, 4, 5 → mean 4.2
- koji (B-003, B-012, B-016, B-020) → 2, 5, 5, 4 → mean 4.0
- kimchi (B-001, B-004, B-009, B-013, B-014, B-017) → 5, 5, 2, 5, 3, 4 → mean 24/6 = 4.0
- kombucha (B-006, B-007) → 3, 4 → mean 3.5
- hot_sauce (B-005, B-015, B-019) → 2, 2, 3 → mean 7/3 ≈ 2.3

Miso wins outright; no tie at the top.

## Gotchas

- Koji and kimchi **tie at 4.0** for second place — easy to misidentify
  one as the winner if you only look at one type's batches.
- The hot_sauce type has the lowest mean (2.3). Notes for hot_sauce
  batches include "peach variant", "fire", and "fermented mash too long"
  — Wren is honest about her weaker batches.
