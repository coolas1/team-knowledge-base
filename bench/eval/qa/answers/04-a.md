# A04 — Deepest typical dive site

## Answer

**RYK-10 (Kerama Outer)** — typical depth **18 m**.

## Sources

- `raw/research/dive-sites.csv` row 11 — `typical_depth_m=18`, `difficulty=advanced`.
- `raw/research/bleaching-survey-2024.xlsx` `Sites` sheet row 11 — `depth_m=18`.

## Reasoning

Scan the `typical_depth_m` column of `dive-sites.csv`:
12, 10, 8, 6, 14, 15, 8, 10, 7, **18**, 11, 9. Max is 18 at RYK-10.
The xlsx `Sites` sheet confirms via its `depth_m` column.

## Gotchas

The depth column appears under two slightly different names — `typical_depth_m`
in the CSV and `depth_m` in the xlsx — but the values match. The deepest
site is also the only one rated `advanced` alongside RYK-05 and RYK-06
(which are 14 and 15 m respectively, both also `advanced`).
