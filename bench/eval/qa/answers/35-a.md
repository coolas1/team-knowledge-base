# A35 — South China Sea survey date range and bleaching trend

## Answer

The survey ran **2024-06 to 2024-09** (summer monsoon). At the warmest site
(永乐环礁东礁 / SCS-01), bleaching rose from **~22% in June** to **~58% in
September**.

## Sources

- `raw/research/scs-bleaching-survey-zh.pdf` §二 (dates: 2024年6月至9月) and
  §四 (22% → 58% at 永乐环礁东礁).
- `raw/research/scs-bleaching-2024-zh.xlsx` sheet 季度白化百分比 — SCS-01 column
  rises from ~22 (2024Q2) to ~58 (2024Q3).
- `raw/research/south-china-sea-coral-zh.md` — mirror.

## Reasoning

1. The Chinese PDF §二 gives the Jun–Sep 2024 window; §四 gives the 22%→58% rise
   at the warmest site.
2. The xlsx quarterly sheet confirms SCS-01 peaks in Q3 (summer).

## Gotchas

- The xlsx uses quarterly buckets (Q2/Q3) while the prose describes monthly
 复查; June ≈ Q2 and September ≈ Q3, so the two granularities agree.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "Jun–Sep 2024; warmest site (SCS-01) rose from ~22% to ~58%."
