# A24 — Maximum SST across all sites and months

## Answer

**31.2 °C** at site **RYK-11 (Amami North)** in **August** (2024).

## Sources

- `raw/research/bleaching-survey-2024.xlsx` → sheet `SST_2024_monthly`,
  row `Aug`, column `RYK-11` → value **31.2**.

## Reasoning

The sheet is 12 rows (months) × 12 columns (sites). The August row is
the warmest month overall; within August, RYK-11 (28.432 °N, Amami-oshima)
is the warmest site at 31.2 °C.

Runner-up: RYK-12 (Amami South) in August at 30.9 °C, and RYK-11 in July
at 30.9 °C. RYK-11 August is the unique maximum.

Note the geographic pattern: northern sites (RYK-11, RYK-12 at Amami)
are *warmer* than expected because the latitude offset in the generator
makes northern sites slightly warmer than the southern ones in this
sheet's data — this is a quirk of the generator's `site_offset` formula
(see `scaffold-personal.py` line 389: `site_offset = {s[0]: (26.5 - s[3]) * -0.8}`),
which produces larger offsets for higher-latitude sites. (Real-world
Ryukyus are warmer at lower latitudes; the synthetic data inverts this.)

## Gotchas

- The latitude offset is **inverted vs the real world** — a careful
  reader who knows Ryukyu geography might expect southern sites (lower
  latitude) to be warmer, but the synthetic data has Amami (28 °N)
  warmer than Yonaguni (24 °N). The generator script is the source of
  truth; don't try to "correct" the answer based on real geography.
- Don't confuse `SST_2024_monthly` (12 months × 12 sites) with
  `Bleaching_quarterly_pct` (20 quarters × 12 sites, in percent units).
  Different units, different shapes.
