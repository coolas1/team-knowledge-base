# A39 — 2024-03-15 Sesoko West temperature: do Wren's records agree?

## Answer

**They disagree.** For the same 2024-03-15 Sesoko West (RYK-06, 15 m) logger
pull:

- English `lab-notebook.md`: **22.7 °C**.
- Japanese field notes (`sesoko-field-notes-ja.pdf` / `.md`): **23.4 °C**.

A ~0.7 °C discrepancy. The correct behavior is to **report both values and flag
the conflict**, not silently pick one.

## Sources

- `raw/research/lab-notebook.md` — "## 2024-03-15 — Logger pull, Sesoko West":
  "Bottom temp (15 m) read **22.7 °C**".
- `raw/research/sesoko-field-notes-ja.pdf` §3 / `sesoko-field-notes-ja.md` —
  "2024年3月15日、水深15mで **23.4°C**".
- `raw/journal/2024-03-20-ja.md` also cites the 23.4 °C (Japanese) value.

## Reasoning

1. Two independent records of the same dive give different temperatures.
2. This is a deliberate corpus inconsistency (like Q09/Q20/Q28/Q29) — the test
   is whether the agent surfaces **both** and flags the disagreement.

## Gotchas

- An agent that finds only one record gives a partial, misleadingly precise
  answer. Full credit requires flagging **both 22.7 °C (EN) and 23.4 °C (JA)**
  and the disagreement.
- Do NOT average them or silently pick one.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "EN lab notebook 22.7 °C vs JA field notes 23.4 °C — they disagree (~0.7 °C); flag the conflict."
- Any answer giving both 22.7 and 23.4 and explicitly noting the discrepancy.
