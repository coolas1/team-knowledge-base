# A40 — Japanese title on the JP-release Tidal EP cover

## Answer

The Japanese-release cover shows the kanji **潮**, read **しお (shio)**, meaning
**"tide"** — the ebb and flow of the tide (the EP's title concept).

## Sources

- `raw/music/tidal-ep-cover-ja.png` — the cover displays 潮 as the title.
- `raw/music/tidal-ep-liner-notes-ja.md` — "日本盤ジャケットのタイトルは漢字一文字
  「潮」… 読みはしお（shio）、意味は潮の満ち引き＝tide".

## Reasoning

1. The cover PNG renders the kanji 潮 (built as a reportlab PDF and rasterized
   via PyMuPDF, since this environment lacks a system CJK font for PIL).
2. The liner notes give the reading (shio) and the meaning (tide).

## Gotchas

- In environments without CJK fonts, the generator falls back to rendering the
  Latin "TIDAL" on the PNG. The canonical Japanese title **潮** remains
  authoritative via the liner notes — credit answers that state 潮 (shio, tide)
  from the prose even when the image fell back.
