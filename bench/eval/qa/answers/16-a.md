# A16 — Days between NSF panel receipt and resubmission entry

## Answer

**6 days** (Tue 2023-11-14 → Mon 2023-11-20).

## Sources

- `raw/journal/2023-11-15.md` — "The NSF panel reviews landed in my inbox at 11:42 PM Tuesday."
- `raw/research/lab-notebook.md` entry dated **2023-11-20 — NSF grant resubmission** — "Three weeks of rewrite hell. See journal entry 2023-11-15 — the original submission got shredded by the panel, mostly on the symbiont community methods. Reframed the metabarcoding protocol around ITS2 primers (sym_VAR_5.8S2 / sym_VAR_REV). That's now §2.4 in the paper."

## Reasoning

Date arithmetic:

- Receipt: 2023-11-14 (Tuesday — confirmed by Gregorian calendar for 2023).
- Resubmission lab entry: 2023-11-20 (Monday).
- Difference: 20 − 14 = **6 days**.

The lab-notebook entry mentions "Three weeks of rewrite hell", which is
colloquial for "an extended period" — not a literal 21-day count. The
literal elapsed time between the panel receipt and the lab entry is 6 days.

## Gotchas

- The lab-notebook prose says "Three weeks of rewrite hell" — that does
  **not** mean 21 days. It's idiomatic. The actual entry date is 6 days
  after the panel receipt, which is the ground-truth answer.
- 2023-11-15 is the *journal* date (when Wren wrote about the panel
  landing); the panel itself landed 2023-11-14. Use 2023-11-14, not
  2023-11-15, as the start date.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "6 days"
- "Six days (Nov 14 → Nov 20, 2023)"
- "From Tuesday Nov 14 to Monday Nov 20 = 6 days"
