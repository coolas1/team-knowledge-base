# A14 — Travel cost and negative savings months

## Answer

Two months: **March and April 2024**.

| Month | `travel_usd` | `savings_usd` |
| ----- | ------------ | ------------- |
| Mar 2024 | $2,800 | **−$1,250** |
| Apr 2024 | $2,800 | **−$897** |

All other 2024 months had `travel_usd` of $0 or $120 and positive savings.

## Sources

- `raw/notes/finance-2024.csv` rows for Mar (month=Mar, travel_usd=2800, savings_usd=-1250) and Apr (travel_usd=2800, savings_usd=-897).
- The sabbatical itself is documented in `raw/travel/okinawa-2024/blog.md`,
  `itinerary.md`, and `expenses.csv` (which carries March-dated receipts).

## Reasoning

Scan `finance-2024.csv`'s `savings_usd` column for negative values — only
Mar and Apr. Both have `travel_usd=2800`, which corresponds to the
Okinawa sabbatical trip (March 1 – April 19, 2024).

## Gotchas

- **`expenses.csv` is partial.** Every row in `expenses.csv` is dated
  March (the generator only emits March receipts even though the trip
  ran into April). April's $2,800 travel cost shows up only as the
  aggregated `travel_usd` line in `finance-2024.csv`, with no
  receipt-level detail in `expenses.csv`. A retrieval agent that only
  reads `expenses.csv` may wrongly conclude the trip cost ~$812 (the
  sum of March receipts) and miss April entirely.
- The blog (`blog.md`) quotes "Total JPY 387,200 ≈ USD 2,616" for the
  trip — about $1,308/month across Mar+Apr, which roughly reconciles
  with the $2,800 monthly aggregate (the monthly figure includes
  non-trip travel overhead, not just Okinawa receipts).

## Acceptable answer variants

- "March (-$1,250) and April (-$897); travel $2,800 each"
- "Mar and Apr 2024; travel_usd=2800 in both; savings=-1250 and -897"
- "March and April, $2,800 travel each, -$1,250 and -$897 savings"
