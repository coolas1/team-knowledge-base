## Gotchas

- The chart was regenerated on 2024-09-30 (per lab-notebook) to use the
  cluster-aware palette. An earlier version (R² = 0.71) is not in the
  corpus — only the new version.
- The cluster labels include the **site-ID ranges** (e.g., RYK-07-12),
  not the site names. To enumerate the actual sites in each cluster you
  have to expand the range against the Sites sheet.
