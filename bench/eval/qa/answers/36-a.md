# A36 — "Coral bleaching" across Wren's three languages

## Answer

- Japanese: **白化** (はっか, hakka).
- Chinese: **白化** (báihuà).

They share the **same written characters (白化)** — identical form in both
languages, different pronunciation.

## Sources

- `raw/notes/multilingual-glossary.md` — row "coral bleaching": 白化 (はっか) /
  白化 (báihuà), flagged "日中同形" (same form in JA and ZH).

## Reasoning

1. The glossary lists coral bleaching as 白化 in both Japanese and Chinese,
   explicitly noting the shared characters.

## Gotchas

- "Coral" itself is NOT shared — it differs (サンゴ in JA vs 珊瑚 in ZH); only
  "bleaching" (白化) has the same written form in both.
