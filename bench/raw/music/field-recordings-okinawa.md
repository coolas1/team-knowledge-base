# Field recordings — Okinawa 2024

> Source WAVs (48 kHz / 24-bit, Zoom H5 + Aquarian H2a hydrophone where noted)
> are not in this corpus because audio isn't part of the benchmark's ingest
> pipeline today. This file is the **transcript + description** that lets a
> text-only RAG agent reason about what each recording contains. All four
> recordings are referenced from [tracklist.csv](tracklist.csv) and used in
> [polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md](polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md).

## Recording 1 — "Sesoko reef hydrophone"

- **Date / location**: 2024-03-08, 06:40 JST, ~200 m offshore of Sesoko West
  (RYK-06), 8 m depth, Aquarian H2a hydrophone on a 3 m cable.
- **Duration**: 14:32
- **Equipment**: Zoom H5, low-cut filter on, manual gain at 4/10.
- **What's in it**: gentle coral snapping-shrimp crackle (the classic
  biological soundscape), distant parrotfish scraping, occasional grunt
  calls from a passing school of *Plectorhinchus*. Around 04:12 a small
  boat passes overhead — audible as a low-frequency thrum that fades over
  ~30 s.
- **Used in**: Track 1, **"Tidal I (incoming)"** (sample 0:30–3:40).
- **Cross-ref**: dive metadata in [dive-sites.csv](../research/dive-sites.csv)
  row RYK-06; reef panorama photo at
  [photos/reef-pano.jpg](../travel/okinawa-2024/photos/reef-pano.jpg).

## Recording 2 — "Makishi morning"

- **Date / location**: 2024-03-15, 07:15 JST, Makishi Public Market, Naha.
- **Duration**: 22:08
- **Equipment**: Zoom H5 with XY capsule, handheld at chest height, foam
  windscreen.
- **What's in it**: vendor calls (mostly Japanese, some Okinawan dialect),
  knife-on-cutting-board rhythms, the whirr of a fish-tank aerator in a
  seafood stall. Most useful section: from 11:40 onward, the rhythmic
  *chunk-chunk-chunk* of an awamori bottling line at a tiny distillery stall
  near the market's north entrance. That loop became the rhythm bed for
  "Kuroshiro".
- **Used in**: Track 3, **"Kuroshio"** — the bottling-line loop at 124 BPM
  (time-stretched from native ~118).
- **Cross-ref**: market photo at [photos/market.jpg](../travel/okinawa-2024/photos/market.jpg).

## Recording 3 — "Sesoko West hydrophone II"

- **Date / location**: 2024-04-02, 14:10 JST, RYK-06 again, 10 m depth.
- **Duration**: 18:54
- **What's in it**: similar biota to Recording 1, but recorded during peak
  afternoon thermal stress — you can hear the difference. The snapping-shrimp
  crackle is sparser; the overall soundscape is "thinner". Two dolphin
  whistle bursts around 09:30.
- **Used in**: Track 4, **"Cladocopium"** (the dedication track for site
  RYK-06). The hydrophone signal is layered under a Digitone pad in D minor
  throughout the second half.
- **Why this matters**: this is the resilience-refugia site documented in
  [coral-resilience-paper.md](../research/coral-resilience-paper.md) §3.3.
  The soundscape difference between Recording 1 (March, cool) and Recording
  3 (April, warming) is audible even to a non-biologist.

## Recording 4 — "Yonaguni drift"

- **Date / location**: 2024-04-18, 10:25 JST, ~500 m off Yonaguni West
  (RYK-01), drift dive, 12 m average depth.
- **Duration**: 26:17 (most of the dive)
- **Equipment**: Zoom H5 in a soft housing, mounted on the BC strap.
- **What's in it**: sustained regulator-breath rhythm (Wren's own breathing —
  14 breaths/min, slightly elevated for the dive), occasional suit-squeeze
  creaks, distant humpback song from 18:00 onward (Yonaguni is on the
  humpback migration corridor in April). The whale song is faint but clearly
  audible after 18:00; it became the structural pivot of "Tidal II (outgoing)".
- **Used in**: Track 5, **"Tidal II (outgoing)"**. Dive photo at
  [photos/dive-1.jpg](../travel/okinawa-2024/photos/dive-1.jpg).

## Processing notes

All four recordings were normalized to -18 LUFS in Adobe Audition before
importing to Ableton. High-pass at 80 Hz to kill handling noise; gentle
de-noise (4 dB reduction) on Recording 3 only. No pitch shifting except
where noted (Recording 2 → "Kuroshiro").
