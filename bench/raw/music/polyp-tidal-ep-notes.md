# Polyp — Tidal EP

> **Polyp** is Wren's ambient music alias. "Tidal" is the second EP, released
> 2024-06-30 on Small Quiet Rooms (Berkeley). Six tracks, ~33 minutes, built
> around field recordings from the Okinawa sabbatical. Cover art:
> [album-cover.png](album-cover.png). Track details in
> [tracklist.csv](tracklist.csv).

## Origin (Nov 2023)

The EP started as a single sketch on 2023-11-22 — see the journal entry
[2023-11-15](../journal/2023-11-15.md) for context (a bad NSF panel week that
sent Wren into the studio for three days). The sketch was a 70-BPM loop in
F# minor built around a single sub-bass pulse and the sound of a SCUBA
inhale. That became track 2, "Polyp".

## Concept

Six tracks, each tied to a specific field recording from the
[Okinawa trip](../travel/okinawa-2024/blog.md). The arc is tidal: incoming
energy in tracks 1–3, peak at track 4 (the 80-BPM "Cladocopium", dedicated
to the resilience-refugia site RYK-06), outgoing resolution in tracks 5–6.

Track-by-track:

- **T1 — Tidal I (incoming)** (62 BPM, F# minor): reef hydrophone at depth,
  recorded 2024-03-08 off Sesoko. Sub-bass pulse throughout.
- **T2 — Polyp** (72 BPM, A minor): SCUBA inhale + dive-boat hull creaks.
  The oldest material on the EP; sketch from 2023-11.
- **T3 — Kuroshio** (68 BPM, C minor): market field recording (see
  [field-recordings-okinawa.md](field-recordings-okinawa.md) §"Makishi morning").
  Rhythm sampled from a bottling line at the awamori distillery.
- **T4 — Cladocopium** (80 BPM, D minor): the centerpiece. Dedicated to site
  RYK-06 (Sesoko West) — the *Cladocopium* C3k-dominated resilience refugium.
  Hydrophone recorded 2024-04-02.
- **T5 — Tidal II ( outgoing)** (58 BPM, F# minor): drift dive off Yonaguni,
  2024-04-18. Note the intentional space in the title — see T1.
- **T6 — Six Weeks (reprise)** (66 BPM, G minor): Naha airport boarding call,
  last day of the trip. Ambient pad.

## Production tools

- DAW: Ableton Live 12
- Field recorder: Zoom H5 + Aquarian H2a hydrophone
- Hardware: Korg MS-20 (sub-bass on T1, T2, T4), Elektron Digitone (pads)
- Plugins: Valhalla Supermassive (reverb), Soundtoys Crystallizer (granular)
- Mastering: Ozone 11, targeted -14 LUFS for streaming

## Live performance

First live outing: [setlist-2024-04-12.md](setlist-2024-04-12.md), Small Quiet
Rooms release party. The 35-minute set included all six Tidal tracks plus
two older Polyp pieces ("Kelp", "Upwelling") as an encore.

## Reception

Modest but warm. ~2,300 monthly Spotify listeners by August; "Cladocopium"
got playlisted on two ambient curators. The most surprising response was
from marine biologist Twitter — a few people recognized the hydrophone
textures. Two colleagues asked whether the EP could be used in a public
outreach reel; that conversation is tracked in
[notes/ideas.md](../notes/ideas.md).
