## What the trip actually changed

I went to Okinawa expecting to confirm a paper. I came back with a paper,
an EP, and a miso crock. The through-line — which I didn't see until this
week — is that all three are about the same thing: *systems that persist
by being more diverse than they need to be*. A resilient reef, a resilient
soundscape, a resilient culture of ferments. Filed under
[notes/ideas.md](../notes/ideas.md) for the eventual next paper / next EP
/ next crock.
