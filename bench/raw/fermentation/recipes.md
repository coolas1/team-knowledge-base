## Kombucha (continuous-brew, 1-gal jar)

**Vessel**: 1-gal glass jar with a tight-weave cloth cover.

**Ingredients**:
- 3.5 L filtered water
- 200 g organic cane sugar
- 6 black tea bags (single-estate Assam preferred) + 2 green tea bags
- 200 mL mature kombucha (starter, pH ~3.0)
- 1 healthy SCOBY (≈100 g)

**Method**:
1. Brew tea at 95 °C, steep 8 min, remove bags.
2. Dissolve sugar, cool to 26 °C.
3. Add starter liquid, then SCOBY (clean hands, no metal).
4. Cover, ferment at 22–26 °C for 7–14 days. Target end pH **3.0–3.4**.
5. Bottle with optional second-ferment flavor (ginger, mango, jasmine) at
   room temp 2–4 days; refrigerate.

**Notes**: temperature > 27 °C pushes it toward vinegar fast. Below 20 °C
it stalls. See batch B-007 in [ph-readings.csv](ph-readings.csv) for a
representative pH trajectory.

## Koji (cedar-tray method)

**Vessel**: cedar tray, 30 × 20 × 5 cm, with a tight cotton cloth cover.

**Ingredients**:
- 500 g polished short-grain rice (Yamada Nishiki preferred)
- 0.5 g *Aspergillus oryzae* spores (koji-kin), from Cold Mountain
- clean water for washing and soaking

**Method**:
1. Wash rice until water runs clear. Soak 8 h. Steam 50 min (kernels
   should feel firm but translucent, not mushy).
2. Cool to 35 °C. Inoculate by dusting spores through a fine tea strainer,
   mixing gently with bare hands (clean).
3. Wrap in cloth, hold at 30–32 °C for 18 h (covered).
4. Unwrap, mix to break up mycelium, spread in cedar tray 3 cm deep.
5. Hold at 28–30 °C for 36–48 h. Turn every 12 h. Done when the tray smells
   sweet (chestnut) and the kernels are covered in white mycelium.

**Notes**: see batch B-013 in [ph-readings.csv](ph-readings.csv). The first
attempt in 2023-06 failed due to a cold room (21 °C); see
[batch-log.xlsx](batch-log.xlsx) B-002 notes.

## Hot sauce (lacto-fermented mash)

**Vessel**: 500 mL swing-top fermentation bottle (or clamp jar).

**Ingredients**:
- 400 g chiles (mix: 50% Fresno, 30% habanero, 20% red jalapeño)
- 30 g garlic cloves
- 20 g sea salt (2% of total veg weight)
- 100 mL filtered water (only if mash is too dry)
- Optional: 100 g mango, peach, or roasted carrot

**Method**:
1. Stem chiles, weigh with garlic. Calculate salt at 2% of veg weight.
2. Pulse in food processor until chunky (not pureed).
3. Pack into vessel, press to submerge. Add water only if mash is dry.
4. Ferment at 22–26 °C for 7–21 days. Target end pH **< 4.0**.
5. Blend smooth, strain if desired, bottle, refrigerate.

**Notes**: mango variant (B-011 in [batch-log.xlsx](batch-log.xlsx)) won
the informal "lab pickle-off" in October 2023.

## Kimchi (mak-style, radish-included)

**Vessel**: 2 L clamp jar.

**Ingredients**:
- 1 medium Napa cabbage (~1 kg)
- 1 Korean radish (mu), ~300 g
- 60 g sea salt (for the cabbage soak)
- 50 g gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes), medium heat
- 30 g fish sauce
- 20 g fermented shrimp (saeu-jeot) — optional, omit for vegan
- 100 g daikon radish matchsticks
- 80 g Korean chives (bu-chu) or scallions, cut 4 cm
- 30 g garlic, 20 g ginger, 1 small onion (paste)

**Method**:
1. Soak cabbage quarters in 6% brine for 3 h, flipping hourly. Rinse 3×.
2. Make paste: blend garlic, ginger, onion, fish sauce, shrimp, gochugaru.
3. Mix paste with radish matchsticks, daikon, chives.
4. Wearing gloves, massage paste into cabbage leaves until evenly coated.
5. Pack into jar, press to submerge. Ferment at 18–22 °C for 3–7 days
   (slower is better), then refrigerate.

**Notes**: see journal [2024-03-01](../journal/2024-03-01.md) — Wren made
this in the Naha guesthouse kitchen using locally-sourced chiles.

## Miso (1-year minimum)

**Vessel**: 5 L ceramic crock with a glass-plate weight and tight lid.

**Ingredients**:
- 1 kg koji rice (from above recipe)
- 500 g soybeans (cooked to soft, mashed)
- 220 g sea salt (12% of combined koji + bean weight)

**Method**:
1. Mash beans while still warm (50 °C). Cool to 35 °C.
2. Mix salt into koji thoroughly; reserve 1 tbsp for the crock surface.
3. Combine all three, pack into crock in layers, pressing out all air.
4. Salt the surface, weight with glass plate, lid loosely.
5. Hold at 25–28 °C for 6–18 months. Stir weekly for the first month;
   monthly thereafter.

**Notes**: Batch B-019 (started 2024-05-10) is at the 6-month mark now
and tasting great — first taste notes are in [batch-log.xlsx](batch-log.xlsx).
Inspired by awamori-distillery visits; see [travel/okinawa-2024/blog.md](../travel/okinawa-2024/blog.md).
