# 2024-03-01 (Friday)

Landed at Naha 16:40 after the long SFO→NRT→OKA chain. The Kuroshio
teleconnection everyone writes about in the climate literature is invisible
from a window seat, but the airport was *warm* — 22 °C and humid at 5 PM,
which my Pacific Northwest body wasn't ready for.

Three observations from the first day:

1. The guesthouse owner (a woman named Tomoko who's been running the place
   since 1987) immediately asked if I was here for the reefs or the
   awamori. I said both and she laughed. We'll get along.
2. The seafood counter at Makishi is overwhelming. Three species I've never
   seen in person, including something labeled "gurukun" that I'm fairly
   sure is the Okinawan word for double-lined mackerel.
3. I forgot how quiet reef-adjacent cities are at night. Santa Cruz has
   constant Highway 1 noise; Naha at 9 PM was just cicadas.

Did not dive today. Did get hungry at 11 PM, walked to a 24-hour konbini,
and ended up buying ingredients to ferment. The Naha konbini has gochugaru,
which I did not expect. Started a small kimchi batch in the guesthouse
kitchen; see the updated recipe in [fermentation/recipes.md](../fermentation/recipes.md).
Will check on it in three days.

Hajime meets me at Sesoko Station on Mar 7. Until then: gear prep, market
mornings, and a lot of reading.
