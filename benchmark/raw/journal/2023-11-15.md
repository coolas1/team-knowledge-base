# 2023-11-15 (Wednesday)

The NSF panel reviews landed in my inbox at 11:42 PM Tuesday. I should not
have opened them before bed. Three reviewers, two of whom clearly didn't
read past the abstract. The third was generous but had specific objections
to the symbiont metabarcoding protocol — and they were right, the protocol
was sloppy.

Spent most of today alternating between redrafting the methods section
([lab-notebook.md](../research/lab-notebook.md) tracks the rewriting) and
sitting at the keyboard without recording anything. Around 8 PM I gave up
on the grant and patched the MS-20 back together.

What came out: a 70 BPM loop in F# minor, just a sub-bass pulse and a
filtered noise bed. I sampled my own SCUBA inhale from the 2022 dive logs
— the recording is from RYK-05 in October 2022, when the water was 18 °C
and my breathing was loud. The sketch is rough but I think there's an EP
in it. Filed under "Polyp / sketch 01".

I don't know if this is avoidance or alchemy. Probably both. Bed.
