# A32 — South China Sea bleaching breakpoint from the Chinese report

## Answer

The Chinese South China Sea report states a bleaching breakpoint of
**DHW = 7.8 度·周**, with a **95% confidence interval of 7.3–8.3**. This is
slightly lower than the Ryukyu breakpoint (≈ 8.36).

## Sources

- `raw/research/scs-bleaching-survey-zh.pdf` §三 / §五 — "白化临界值…断点为
  DHW = 7.8 度·周（95% CI 7.3–8.3）".
- `raw/research/south-china-sea-coral-zh.md` — markdown mirror.

## Reasoning

1. The Chinese PDF §三 states the breakpoint 7.8 with CI 7.3–8.3.
2. §五 contrasts it with the Ryukyu value 8.36.

## Gotchas

- Do NOT confuse with the Ryukyu paper's breakpoint of 8.36 (95% CI 7.91–8.82)
  — that figure lives in the **English** paper and is a different dataset.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "DHW ≈ 7.8 (95% CI 7.3–8.3)"
- "7.8 度·周, CI 7.3–8.3"
