# A06 — Koji fungus and rice variety

## Answer

Inoculant: ***Aspergillus oryzae*** (referred to as **koji-kin**; sourced
from Cold Mountain in the US, with a warm-climate-adapted Okinawan strain
from Nago added later in 2024). Preferred rice: **Yamada Nishiki** (polished
short-grain).

## Sources

- `raw/fermentation/recipes.md` §"Koji (cedar-tray method)" — Ingredients list:
  - "500 g polished short-grain rice (Yamada Nishiki preferred)"
  - "0.5 g *Aspergillus oryzae* spores (koji-kin), from Cold Mountain"
- `raw/travel/okinawa-2024/blog.md` §"The jars" — confirms the Nago
  koji-kin acquisition (warm-climate-adapted, ~80 years).

## Reasoning

Direct ingredient lookup. The recipes.md koji section lists both items
explicitly.

## Gotchas

The Cold Mountain strain is the US baseline; the Nago strain is a 2024
sabbatical acquisition used for batch B-018 onward. The question asks
about the recipe as written, which names Cold Mountain.
