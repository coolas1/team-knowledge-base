## Gotchas

- **The JPG itself is generic.** `dive-1.jpg` is a Picsum stock image
  (seed `okinawa-dive-1`) whose pixel content is arbitrary and has no
  semantic relationship to Yonaguni. A vision-only RAG describing the
  image will not know it's RYK-01 — that semantic meaning lives only in
  the surrounding prose. **This is by design** (see `STORY.md`: "Picsum
  returns generic stock images; the surrounding prose supplies the
  semantic content").
- Don't confuse with `reef-pano.jpg` (also under `photos/`) which the
  blog attributes to a shallower survey at **Sesoko** (RYK-06), not
  Yonaguni.
- Note the intentional space in the track title: "Tidal II ( outgoing)".
