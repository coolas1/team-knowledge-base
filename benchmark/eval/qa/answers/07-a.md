# A07 — Three text lines on the Tidal album cover

## Answer

Top to bottom:

1. **"TIDAL"** — large white title text, centered, near the upper third.
2. **"POLYP"** — medium light-gray-blue text, centered, just below "TIDAL".
3. **"2024  ·  FIELD RECORDINGS"** — small dim text, centered, near the
   bottom of the cover.

## Sources

- `raw/music/album-cover.png` — OCR / vision read of the image.
- `scripts/generation/scaffold-personal.py` `write_album_cover` function —
  the three `center_text(...)` calls, in source order, are:
  1. `center_text("TIDAL", f_title, 180, "white")` — size 74 bold
  2. `center_text("POLYP", f_sub, 270, (210, 220, 230))` — size 30 bold
  3. `center_text("2024  ·  FIELD RECORDINGS", f_meta, 720, (170, 180, 190))` — size 14 bold

## Reasoning

Either OCR the image directly or read the generator source. The three
calls appear in the order they're drawn; the y-coordinates (180, 270, 720)
match top-to-bottom placement on the 800×800 canvas.

## Gotchas

The bullet separator between "2024" and "FIELD RECORDINGS" is a middle-dot
(U+00B7), not a regular period. Note also the double space around it —
that's how the generator writes it.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "TIDAL / POLYP / 2024 · FIELD RECORDINGS"
- "TIDAL (top), POLYP (middle), 2024 · FIELD RECORDINGS (bottom)"
- "TIDAL; POLYP; 2024 FIELD RECORDINGS" (separator may be dropped)
