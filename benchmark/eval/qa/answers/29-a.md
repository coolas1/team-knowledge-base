# A29 — Okinawa trip total JPY: blog vs expenses.csv

## Answer

**No, they are not consistent.** The blog states the full trip total;
the CSV captures only a partial subset of receipts.

| Source | Total JPY | Total USD (≈) | What it represents |
| ------ | --------- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| `blog.md` | **JPY 387,200** | **USD ~2,616** | Narrative total of the full six-week trip (Mar 1 – Apr 19, 2024) |
| `expenses.csv` (sum of `amount_jpy` column) | **JPY 120,200** | **USD ~812** | Sum of 19 receipt line items (lodging, food, dive, transit, gear, music, fermentation); all dated in March |

Conversion rate used in `expenses.csv`: **148 JPY/USD** (per
`scaffold-personal.py` `write_csvs`).

## Sources

- `raw/travel/okinawa-2024/blog.md` §"What it cost":
  > "Total JPY 387,200 ≈ USD 2,616 at the time. Mostly lodging (six
  > weeks adds up) and dive charters."
- `raw/travel/okinawa-2024/expenses.csv` — 19 data rows; sum of
  `amount_jpy` column = 120,200 (verifiable by direct summation).

## Reasoning

Sum every `amount_jpy` value in `expenses.csv`:

```
5500 + 12000 + 6800 + 1800 + 4500 + 650 + 18000 + 12000 + 22000
+ 14000 + 700 + 4500 + 2200 + 1400 + 850 + 1800 + 6500 + 1500 + 3500
= 120,200 JPY
```

Convert: 120,200 / 148 = **$812.16 USD**.

The blog claims 387,200 JPY ≈ $2,616 USD. The CSV is short by
~JPY 267,000 / ~USD 1,804.

## Gotchas

**Documented inconsistency.** Full credit requires surfacing both
totals and explaining the discrepancy — not silently picking one.

What's going on:

1. **`expenses.csv` is partial.** Every row is dated in March 2024
   (the generator only emits March receipts even though the trip ran
   through April 19). April's spending is missing entirely.
2. **`expenses.csv` only covers 7 vendor categories × 2–3 entries each
   = 19 receipts.** A real six-week trip would generate dozens more.
   The CSV is illustrative, not exhaustive.
3. **The blog figure ($2,616) reconciles with the aggregated monthly
   `travel_usd` line in `notes/finance-2024.csv`**: March travel = $2,800
   and April travel = $2,800, totaling $5,600 across both months.
   The blog's $2,616 is about half of that — plausible for the Okinawa
   share of Mar+Apr travel spending (the rest being non-Okinawa travel).
4. **The CSV sum ($812) is the receipt-level ground truth for what it
   covers** (March only, 7 categories, 19 entries). It is NOT the trip
   total.

A retrieval agent that reports only "$2,616" or only "$812" is partial
credit. The right answer names both and explains.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "Inconsistent: blog says JPY 387,200 (USD ~2,616) for the full trip; CSV sums to JPY 120,200 (USD ~812) across 19 March-only receipts. CSV is partial."
- "blog = JPY 387,200 / USD 2,616 (full trip); expenses.csv = JPY 120,200 / USD 812 (19 receipts, March only). Discrepancy explained by CSV being partial."
- Any answer that reports both numbers and identifies the CSV as a partial record.
