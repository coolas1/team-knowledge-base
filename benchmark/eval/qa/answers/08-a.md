Track 4, **"Cladocopium"**, dedicated to site **RYK-06 (Sesoko West)**.

## Sources

- `raw/music/polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md` §T4 — "the centerpiece. Dedicated to site RYK-06 (Sesoko West) — the *Cladocopium* C3k-dominated resilience refugium. Hydrophone recorded 2024-04-02."

## Reasoning

1. Start at `tracklist.csv`. Only one row has "dedicated to refugia site" in its `notes` — row 5 (track 4 "Cladocopium").
2. Cross-check via `polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md` §T4 prose, which names RYK-06 explicitly.
3. To verify RYK-06 is *actually* a paper refugium (not just an EP claim), open the paper §3.3 and the quarterly bleaching sheet — both confirm RYK-06 stays under ~20% bleaching through 2023.

None. The four-source chain is internally consistent. Note that RYK-05
(Cape Hedo) is the *other* refugium per the paper, but the EP dedicates
Cladocopium specifically to RYK-06 — not both.
