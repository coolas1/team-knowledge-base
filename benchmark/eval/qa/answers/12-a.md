# A12 — SCUBA inhale sample: site and track

## Answer

Sample source: **RYK-05 (Cape Hedo)**, October 2022 dive, water
temperature 18 °C. Used in: **Track 2, "Polyp"**.

## Sources

- `raw/journal/2023-11-15.md` — "I sampled my own SCUBA inhale from the 2022 dive logs — the recording is from RYK-05 in October 2022, when the water was 18 °C and my breathing was loud."
- `raw/music/polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md` §"Origin" — the sketch "built around a single sub-bass pulse and the sound of a SCUBA inhale. That became track 2, 'Polyp'."
- `raw/music/polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md` §T2 — "T2 — Polyp … SCUBA inhale + dive-boat hull creaks. The oldest material on the EP; sketch from 2023-11."
- `raw/music/tracklist.csv` row 3 — T2 "Polyp", `primary_samples="SCUBA inhale, dive boat hull creaks"`.

## Reasoning

The journal names the dive site (RYK-05) and conditions (Oct 2022, 18 °C)
directly. The EP notes' §Origin and §T2 both confirm the SCUBA-inhale
sketch became T2 "Polyp". tracklist.csv confirms the sample credit.

## Gotchas

A retrieval agent that only reads tracklist.csv will see "SCUBA inhale"
but not which dive site it came from — that detail is only in the journal.
This question forces a journal↔EP-notes cross-reference.

The 2022-10 dive at RYK-05 is also distinct from the 2024 sabbatical dives
(2024-03-08 at RYK-06, etc.). Don't confuse them.
