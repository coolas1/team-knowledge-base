# A19 — Okinawa sabbatical start and end dates

## Answer

**March 1, 2024 → April 19, 2024** (six weeks, fly-out and fly-home
inclusive).

## Sources

- `raw/travel/okinawa-2024/blog.md` opening blockquote: "**Okinawa, March 1 – April 19, 2024.** Six weeks."
- `raw/journal/2024-03-01.md` — "Landed at Naha 16:40 …" (arrival day diary).
- `raw/travel/okinawa-2024/itinerary.md` Week 6 row "Apr 19" — "OKA→NRT→SFO; home".

## Reasoning

Three independent sources all confirm the same start (Mar 1) and end
(Apr 19). The arrival journal entry is dated Mar 1; the itinerary's last
row is Apr 19 with the "home" flight chain.

## Gotchas

- The trip is described as "six weeks" but is actually 50 days (Mar 1 →
  Apr 19 inclusive), which is 7 weeks + 1 day. The "six weeks" framing is
  approximate.
- April 19, 2024 was a Friday; March 1, 2024 was a Friday too.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "Mar 1, 2024 → Apr 19, 2024"
- "2024-03-01 to 2024-04-19"
- "March 1 through April 19, 2024"
