# A20 — Date of B-019's 100-day mark

## Answer

**Tuesday, 2024-08-20**.

(2024-08-22 was a Thursday; the journal entry says "Tuesday", which is
the Tuesday of that same week = Aug 20.)

## Sources

- `raw/journal/2024-08-22.md` §"Miso update" — "B-019 hit the 100-day mark on Tuesday."

## Reasoning

The journal entry header is dated 2024-08-22. 2024-08-22 was a Thursday
(per Gregorian calendar). The Tuesday of that week (2 days earlier) is
**2024-08-20**.

A back-of-the-envelope cross-check: if B-019 hit 100 days on Aug 20,
2024, it started ~May 12, 2024. This aligns with the prose sources
(`recipes.md` says "Batch B-019 (started 2024-05-10)"; `journal/2024-05-08.md`
says "Miso B-019 is at 4 weeks" — implying a ~April 10 start, which is
loosely consistent with a "first taste at 90 days" milestone narrative).

## Gotchas

**Documented inconsistency about B-019's type and start date.** The
corpus does NOT agree on what B-019 actually is:

| Source | B-019 type | B-019 start_date | Notes |
| ------ | ---------- | ---------------- | ----- |
| `batch-log.xlsx` `Batches` sheet | `hot_sauce` | `2024-02-13` | 10-day duration |
| `recipes.md` Miso section | miso | `2024-05-10` | "Batch B-019 (started 2024-05-10) is at the 6-month mark" |
| `journal/2024-05-08.md` Ferments | miso | (implied ~April 10) | "Miso B-019 is at 4 weeks" on May 8 |
| `journal/2024-08-22.md` Miso update | miso | (implied ~May 12) | "100-day mark" on Tue Aug 20 |

A retrieval agent should surface this discrepancy, not silently pick one
side. The preponderance of prose evidence (3 sources, including the
file that names B-019 in its Miso section) says **miso started early May
2024**; the xlsx row alone says **hot_sauce started Feb 13**. The xlsx
appears to be wrong about B-019 — likely a generator artifact (the script
randomly assigns batch types via `RNG.choice(types)` and the random
draw produced `hot_sauce` for the B-019 row, but the prose was written
to refer to B-019 as the miso batch).
