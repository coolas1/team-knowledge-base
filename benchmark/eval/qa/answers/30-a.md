# A30 — Cluster at low-bleach despite high SST, and paper §3.3 cross-ref

## Answer

The cluster is **"Northern refugia (RYK-05,06)"** (dark blue in the
chart legend) — it sits in the upper-SST range but stays near the bottom
of the bleaching axis (single-digit to ~15%).

This corresponds to **paper §3.3 — "Resilience outliers"**, which names
**Cape Hedo (RYK-05)** and **Sesoko West (RYK-06)** as the two
resilience-refugium sites that "maintained mean bleaching < 20%
throughout 2023 despite DHW > 10 at both."

## Reasoning

1. The chart's three clusters have visually distinct bleach/SST ranges.
   The "Northern refugia" cluster (RYK-05,06) is the obvious outlier:
   low bleach despite SST comparable to the mid-latitude cluster.
2. Paper §3.3 names exactly these two sites as the resilience outliers
   and attributes their resistance to persistent *Cladocopium* C3k
   symbiont dominance (vs the typical *Durusdinium* shuffle).
3. The xlsx quarterly data confirms the refugium pattern holds across
   the full 20-quarter time series, not just visually on the chart.

## Gotchas

- Don't confuse this cluster's position with the linear trend line —
  the trend line fits **all 40 points pooled**, and the refugia cluster
  sits **below** the trend (positive residuals in SST, negative in
  bleaching). That visual "below-trend" position is what makes them
  outliers.
- The cluster color (dark blue, `#2c7fb8`) is distinct from the
  mid-latitude cluster's cyan (`#41b6c4`) — easy to confuse at small
  chart sizes. The label in the legend is the disambiguator.
