# A13 — Triad: refugium AND EP sample AND journal inhale

## Answer

**RYK-05 (Cape Hedo)** is the only site that satisfies all three
conditions.

| # | Condition | How RYK-05 satisfies it |
| - | --------- | ----------------------- |
| 1 | Paper refugium | `coral-resilience-paper.md` §3.3 lists Cape Hedo (RYK-05) and Sesoko West (RYK-06) as the two refugia. |
| 2 | EP sample credit | `journal/2023-11-15.md` and `polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md` §Origin both say the SCUBA inhale sample (recorded at RYK-05 in Oct 2022) is part of Track 2 "Polyp". `tracklist.csv` row 3 lists "SCUBA inhale, dive boat hull creaks" as the primary sample. |
| 3 | 2023-11-15 journal references 2022 dive there | The journal entry names RYK-05 explicitly: "the recording is from RYK-05 in October 2022, when the water was 18 °C." |

## Sources

- `raw/research/coral-resilience-paper.md` §3.3 ("Two northern forereef sites—Cape Hedo and Sesoko West—maintained mean bleaching < 20% throughout 2023 …")
- `raw/research/coral-resilience-paper.md` Table 1 (mirrored in `scaffold-personal.py` `PAPER["table_rows"]`) — RYK-05 row: refugia_flag = "yes".
- `raw/journal/2023-11-15.md` — explicit RYK-05 reference.
- `raw/music/polyp-tidal-ep-notes.md` §Origin and §T2 — sketch → T2 Polyp chain.

## Reasoning

The two paper refugia are RYK-05 and RYK-06. Cross-checking each against
the other two conditions:

- **RYK-05**: journal says the 2022 SCUBA inhale sample came from here;
  ep-notes confirm that sample is in T2 Polyp. ✓ All three.
- **RYK-06**: EP credits RYK-06 in T1 (Sesoko hydrophone 2024-03-08) and
  T4 (Cladocopium 2024-04-02), but the 2023-11-15 journal's SCUBA inhale
  sample is from RYK-05, not RYK-06. ✗ Fails condition 3.

So only RYK-05 satisfies all three.

## Gotchas

It's tempting to answer RYK-06 because that site has the most EP coverage
(both T1 and T4 reference it). But the journal condition specifically
requires the 2022 SCUBA inhale sample, which is from RYK-05. The triad
constraint is what makes this question hard.
