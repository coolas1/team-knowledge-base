# A05 — When the NSF panel reviews landed

## Answer

**11:42 PM on Tuesday, 2023-11-14**. (The journal entry is dated Wednesday
2023-11-15 — the day after — but the reviews arrived the previous night.)

## Sources

- `raw/journal/2023-11-15.md` first paragraph: "The NSF panel reviews
  landed in my inbox at 11:42 PM Tuesday."

## Reasoning

The journal entry is written on Wednesday 2023-11-15 and refers back to
"11:42 PM Tuesday" — the immediately preceding Tuesday is 2023-11-14.
This is one day before the entry date.

## Gotchas

- A retrieval agent that only reads the entry's frontmatter `created: 2023-11-15`
  and the file name may wrongly report 2023-11-15 as the receipt date.
  The receipt was the night before; 2023-11-15 is when Wren wrote about it.
- 2023-11-14 was indeed a Tuesday (and 2023-11-15 a Wednesday) per the
  Gregorian calendar.

## Acceptable answer variants

- "11:42 PM on Tuesday Nov 14, 2023"
- "Tue 2023-11-14, 23:42"
- "Tuesday night 2023-11-14 at 11:42 PM"
