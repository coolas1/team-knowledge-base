# Reading list — books in active rotation

> Updated quarterly. Stars mark books I'd actively recommend; the absence of a
> star doesn't mean bad, just that I wouldn't push it on someone unprompted.

## Coral / reef science

- **\*** Hughes et al. (2017), *Global warming and recurrent mass bleaching
  of corals* (Nature 543). Foundational. The paper everyone cites; worth
  reading in full.
- **\*** Dietzel et al. (2020), *Long-term shifts in mass coral bleaching
  mortality* (Proc Roy Soc B). The model underlying most of my DHW analysis.
- **\*** Boulotte et al. (2016), *Exploring the Symbiodinium rare biosphere*.
  The paper that reframed how I think about symbiont communities. The
  resilience-refugia framing in
  [research/coral-resilience-paper.md](../research/coral-resilience-paper.md)
  §4 is essentially an application of Boulotte's argument.
- Cunning et al. (2018), *Warming patterns associated with symbiont
  shuffling*. Useful but the cnidarian model is over-simplified.
- **\*** Putnam & Gates (2015), *Building coral reef resilience through
  assisted evolution* (PNAS). Controversial when it came out; less so now.
- Van Oppen et al. (2015), same volume. Read them together.

## Music / sound

- **\*** Schafer (1977), *The Soundscape*. Cliché to recommend but it's
  foundational for a reason. Re-read before every EP.
- **\*** Toop (2004), *Haunted Weather*. The book that made me start
  recording ambient music seriously.
- Labelle (2010), *Background Noise*. Denser than Toop; useful for theory.
- Cascone (2000), *The Aesthetics of Failure* (essay). Read it once a year.
- Field recording practice: Wikipedia is honestly the best free starting
  point; the Hydrophone Wikipedia article is more useful than most
  textbooks.

## Fermentation / food

- **\*** Katz (2012), *The Art of Fermentation*. The reference. Everything
  else is a footnote. See especially the koji chapter, which informed
  [fermentation/recipes.md](../fermentation/recipes.md).
- **\*** Shih (2020), *Koji Alchemy*. Tighter than Katz on koji
  specifically; the cold-climate adaptation discussion is relevant to my
  Nago-vs-Cold-Mountain comparison.
- Nomura (2014), *Sushi, Teppanyaki, Tempura, Yakitori* (history). Not
  strictly fermentation but useful context for the awamori / shochu
  lineages.
- Nakamura & Trichopoulos (2016), *Miso: A Culinary History of Japan's
  National Seasoning*. Mostly for the bibliography.

## General / unrelated

- **\*** Le Guin (1969), *The Left Hand of Darkness*. Reread in 2024-Q2.
  Always worth it.
- **\*** Kimmerer (2013), *Braiding Sweetgrass*. I am the last person in
  the Western Hemisphere to read this, but yes it's good.
- Strogatz (2003), *Sync*. Re-read for the EP title concept; the tidal
  synchronization chapter is more relevant than the title suggests.
- Carson (1951), *The Sea Around Us*. A reminder to slow down when
  writing.

## Backlog (not yet started)

- Mukherjee (2010), *The Emperor of All Maladies*. Intimidating length.
