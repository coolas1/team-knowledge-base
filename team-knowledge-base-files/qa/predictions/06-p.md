# P06 — Koji fungus and rice variety

## Answer
Inoculant: *Aspergillus oryzae* spores (koji-kin), sourced from Cold Mountain (per recipes.md; the travel blog adds a Nago brewer's warm-climate-adapted strain of the same species). Preferred rice: polished short-grain rice, **Yamada Nishiki preferred** (500 g in the cedar-tray method).
